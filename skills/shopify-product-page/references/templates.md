```yaml
approved_promise:

offer:

required_product_facts:
restricted_claims:

```

## Above-the-fold draft

```text
Product title

One-sentence approved benefit or use case

Price, currency, and any truthful recurring-purchase terms

Variant and quantity controls with clear availability

Primary purchase action

One or two decision-critical facts, such as a verified delivery range,
return condition, specification, or proof statement
```

## Benefit block

```text
Benefit heading

Plain-language explanation of why the feature matters for the named use case.

Proof or specification: [source-backed fact]
Qualification: [limit or condition when needed]
```

## FAQ record

```yaml
question:
answer:
source:
last_reviewed:
owner:
page_placement:
```

If the answer depends on market, variant, location, or order state, make the condition explicit.

## Handoff checklist

- every product fact maps to a source
- price, variant, inventory, subscription, and offer logic are current
- proof is real, permissioned, and placed near the supported claim
- shipping, returns, warranty, and guarantee match policy
- headings and controls remain accessible
- mobile order and sticky elements are reviewed
- metadata and structured data are handled by the correct system
- tracking events and consent behavior remain intact
- draft, saved, published, live, and verified states are recorded
