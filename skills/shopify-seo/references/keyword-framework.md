# Query and Page Framework

## Query evidence

Prefer current first-party Search Console query and page data, then supplement with credible research tools and live result inspection when available. Label estimated volume and difficulty as estimates with source and date.

## Page-type choices

- **Product:** a specific product or variant best satisfies the intent.
- **Collection:** the user needs selection, filtering, or comparison across products.
- **Guide or article:** the user needs education, diagnosis, instructions, or comparison before a commercial page.
- **Policy or support:** the task concerns shipping, returns, care, sizing, compatibility, or service information.
- **Homepage or brand page:** navigational or brand-level intent.

Assign one primary destination per intent and market unless distinct pages satisfy materially different tasks. Do not target the same intent across several pages only to create more content.

## On-page evidence

Align title, primary heading, copy, product or collection content, metadata, media, internal anchors, and structured data with the actual page. Preserve readability and product truth. Keyword repetition is not a substitute for satisfying intent.

## Cannibalization review

Check whether multiple URLs receive impressions for the same query and whether the pages serve the same task. Different URLs appearing for a broad query are not automatically cannibalization. Review canonical state, internal links, content purpose, backlinks, conversion role, and trend before merging or redirecting.
