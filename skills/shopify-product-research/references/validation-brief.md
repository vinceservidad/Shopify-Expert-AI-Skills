## Recommendation states

- **Reject:** a rejection condition is met or the opportunity cannot support the required constraints.
- **Research further:** a decision-changing unknown can be resolved cheaply.
- **Sample:** physical quality, fit, packaging, or supplier capability must be tested.
- **Demand test:** customer interest or willingness to pay remains uncertain.
- **Pilot:** evidence supports a bounded operational and commercial trial.
- **Proceed:** the named readiness gates pass for the verified scope.

“Proceed” is not a forecast or guarantee.


Do not take payment for an unavailable or unapproved offer without truthful terms and authorized fulfillment capability.

## Supplier question set

Ask only the questions relevant to the product: legal identity, facility, specification, materials, sample, quality system, defect policy, certification source, testing, capacity, minimum order, lead time, payment, packaging, labeling, customization, incoterms, shipping, documentation, and change control. Independently verify decision-critical claims.
