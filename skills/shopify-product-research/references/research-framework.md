## Evidence map

### Customer

- interviews, surveys, reviews, support, returns, on-site search, wish lists, wait lists, and repeat behavior
- exact quotation, source, date, population, and permission when quoting
- problem frequency, severity, current alternative, switching cost, and purchase trigger

### Demand

- store search and sales history
- search-query and keyword evidence
- retailer and marketplace availability, reviews, price, and stock movement when observable
- ad-library or creative patterns as market activity, not proof of profitable sales
- community questions and trends with source and sample limits

### Competition and alternatives

- product, specification, price, offer, proof, shipping, return, review themes, positioning, channel, and customer experience
- direct competitors, substitutes, do-it-yourself behavior, and non-consumption

### Supply and operations

- specifications, sample, minimum order, unit cost, lead time, capacity, payment terms, packaging, quality process, certifications, incoterms, freight, duty, defects, returns, and contingency
- verify suppliers through appropriate independent checks before relying on identity or capability

## Economics

Model a named outcome, such as contribution profit after product cost, inbound freight, duty, packaging, payment fees, fulfillment, shipping subsidy, discounts, returns, and media cost.

Use sensitivity ranges for uncertain inputs. Do not hide a negative case behind one optimistic point estimate.

## Risk screen

- regulated or safety-sensitive category
- medical, cosmetic, environmental, comparative, performance, or certification claims
- intellectual property and counterfeit risk
- fragile, hazardous, perishable, oversized, temperature-sensitive, or restricted shipping
- high defect, return, support, installation, or education burden
- seasonality, trend dependence, supplier concentration, inventory obsolescence, and cash cycle

Escalate legal, regulatory, certification, safety, or supplier-verification decisions to qualified owners.
