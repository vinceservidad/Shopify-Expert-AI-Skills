# Support Response Guidelines

## Evidence record

```yaml
customer_statement:
verified_order_facts:
carrier_or_fulfillment_facts:
applicable_policy_and_version:
previous_actions:
unknowns:
authorized_remedies:
escalation_owner:
```

Customer statements are important evidence of experience, but they do not automatically establish the system or policy state.

## Response structure

1. Acknowledge the specific issue without admitting an unverified cause.
2. State only the facts that are useful and safe to share.
3. Explain the action already verified as complete, or the next action that will be taken after approval.
4. Give a truthful timeframe only when supported by policy, carrier, operations, or an authorized owner.
5. Ask for the minimum additional information needed.
6. Close with a clear next step.

## Safe language

Prefer:

```text
I can see that the parcel was last scanned on [verified date]. I am checking the
next available step under our delivery policy and will update you by [supported
timeframe].
```

Avoid:

```text
It will definitely arrive tomorrow and your refund has already been processed.
```

unless both claims are verified and authorized.

## Operational action record

```yaml
proposed_action:
policy_basis:
authority_required:
target_order_or_record:
customer_impact:
financial_impact:
rollback_or_correction:
verification:
```

## Escalation signals

Follow the merchant's approved route for threats to safety, legal demands, privacy or data requests, payment disputes, suspected fraud, regulated product concerns, discrimination or harassment, repeated failed remedies, media or public escalation, and exceptions beyond the agent's authority.

Do not use a generic escalation label when an immediate safety or legal process is required.
