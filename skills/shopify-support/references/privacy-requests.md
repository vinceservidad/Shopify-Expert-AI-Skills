# Customer Data and Privacy Requests

Use this when a customer asks for a copy of their data, a correction, or deletion of their personal data. These requests have legal deadlines that depend on where the customer lives. The merchant owns the decision; the VA prepares and tracks the request. This is not legal advice.

## Prerequisites

- The merchant's privacy policy and their process for data requests.
- The deadline that applies, confirmed by the merchant. For example, the EU and UK GDPR allow one month, and California's CCPA allows 45 days.
- Where else the customer's data lives: email and SMS apps, reviews, loyalty, help desk, and the 3PL.

## Ordered steps

1. **Log the request.** Record the date received, the channel, the type (access, correction or deletion) and the deadline.
2. **Verify identity.** Reply from the store's support channel to the email on the customer record. Never send data to a different address, and never ask for card numbers.
3. **Check for holds.** Open orders, active disputes or chargebacks, and records the merchant must keep (for example tax records) can delay or limit deletion. Note them for the owner.
4. **Prepare the action.** Find the customer record and use Shopify's customer privacy actions. Labels change, so confirm them in admin; they currently sit under the customer's More actions menu. List the other apps holding this person's data.
5. **Get the owner's approval.** Then the owner, or the VA with written approval, submits the request in Shopify and each app.
6. **Confirm and close.** Tell the customer what was done and what was kept, and why. Record the completion date.

## Stop gates

- No data is sent and nothing is erased before identity is verified and the owner approves.
- Stop and escalate if the request mentions a lawyer, a regulator or a deadline already passed.

## Privacy

Keep the request log free of extra personal data: request ID, dates, type, status and owner. Don't paste customer data into AI chats beyond what the task needs.
