# Store Audit Checklist

Use only the sections relevant to the objective. This is coverage guidance, not proof that every checked item is a problem.

## Global

- domain, security, redirects, canonical host, locale, currency, and market behavior
- header, navigation, search, account, cart, announcements, footer, contact, and policies
- typography, contrast, focus, keyboard operation, labels, errors, zoom, motion, and alternative text
- mobile layout, tap targets, sticky elements, overlays, banners, and app collisions
- third-party scripts, app embeds, consent, tags, and visible performance symptoms

## Homepage and landing pages

- audience and offer clarity above the fold
- message continuity from major acquisition sources
- path to hero products or collections
- proof with visible source or provenance
- merchandising logic, availability, and seasonal relevance
- repeated or competing calls to action

## Collections and search

- collection purpose, titles, copy, filters, sorting, pagination, and empty states
- product-card title, image, price, variant, rating, availability, and badges
- search relevance, typo handling, suggestions, no-result recovery, and tracking
- crawlable and canonical behavior for filtered or parameterized states when SEO is in scope

## Product pages

- title, media, variant, price, quantity, availability, and add-to-cart behavior
- benefits, specifications, use, care, ingredients or materials, and claim support
- shipping, delivery, returns, subscription, warranty, and guarantee truth
- proof, reviews, questions, comparisons, bundles, upsells, and recommendations
- sticky controls, image behavior, long content, errors, and mobile order of information

## Cart and checkout

- cart contents, edit controls, discounts, shipping expectations, taxes, and totals
- upsell relevance and ability to proceed without distraction
- accelerated payment behavior and error recovery
- checkout field burden, localization, payment, delivery, validation, and policy access
- test-order limitations and the evidence needed when checkout cannot be completed

## Post-purchase and retention

- confirmation, order status, tracking, self-service, support, and expectation setting
- account activation, returns, review request, replenishment, cross-sell, and consent
- consistency between transactional messages and store policy

## Technical and measurement

- theme architecture, duplicate templates, unused assets, app code, and console or network errors
- image sizing, responsive sources, lazy loading, font loading, JavaScript execution, and layout shift
- structured data, metadata, canonicals, robots directives, sitemap, redirects, and broken links
- consent behavior, event naming, purchase value, currency, deduplication, and conversion-action settings
- Shopify, GA4, ad-platform, email, and backend reconciliation

## Operations

- inventory truth, backorders, lead time, fulfillment, shipping zones, returns, fraud, tax, and support capacity
- ownership of proposed fixes and ability to maintain them
- rollback, monitoring, and incident handling
