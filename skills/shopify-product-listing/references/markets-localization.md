# Markets and Localized Listings

Use this when products sell in more than one country or language: translated titles and descriptions, market prices, currency display, and which products each market can buy. Market and translation settings change often. Confirm the current labels in the store's Markets and language settings before editing.

## Prerequisites

- The store's markets, their currencies and languages, and the domain or subfolder each one uses.
- How translations are managed: Shopify's translation tools, a translation app, or a translator.
- Approved translated copy or an approved glossary for brand terms, sizes and care words. Never publish machine translation as approved copy without the client's sign-off.
- Market pricing rules: automatic conversion with rounding, or fixed prices per market.
- Restrictions: products that can't ship to, or be sold in, a market.

## Ordered steps

1. **Pick the source listing.** It must be final and approved. Translate only finished copy.
2. **Translate fields in order:** title, description, SEO title and description, image alt text, option names and values, and the metafields shown on the page.
3. **Check units and claims.** Convert sizes and measurements where the market expects other units. Make sure translated claims don't say more than the original.
4. **Check price per market.** Compare the market price with the approved rule for 3 representative products, including rounding and compare-at prices.
5. **Check availability.** Products must be included in or excluded from each market as approved.
6. **Preview each market** in its language and currency. Check the product page, cart and a test checkout up to payment.

## Stop gates

- No publishing of translated copy without the client's approval for that language.
- Stop if a product is restricted in a market and still appears there.

## QA and handoff

For each market and language, list which products are translated, priced and previewed, the reviewer, open issues, and what is live versus draft.
