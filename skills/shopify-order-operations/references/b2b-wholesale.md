# B2B and Wholesale Orders

Use this for wholesale buyers: company accounts, catalogs with special prices, payment terms, draft orders and minimum quantities. Native B2B features depend on the Shopify plan, and some stores use a wholesale app instead. Confirm which setup the store has before following any step.

## Prerequisites

- How wholesale runs in this store: native B2B (companies, catalogs, price lists), a wholesale app, or manual draft orders.
- The client's wholesale terms: price list or discount, minimums, payment terms (for example net 30), tax exemption rules, shipping rules.
- Approval rules: who approves new buyers, credit terms, and orders over a set value.

## Ordered steps

1. **New buyer.** Collect the company name, buyer contacts, location, tax status and documents the client requires. Draft the company or customer record. Don't approve credit terms or tax exemption without the owner.
2. **Catalog and price.** Check that the buyer's location has the right catalog or price list. Spot-check 3 SKUs: compare the price the buyer sees with the approved list.
3. **Order.** For a draft order, add items and confirm that minimums and case packs are met. Apply only approved prices and terms. Send the invoice or checkout link only after approval.
4. **Payment terms.** Track the due date. Report overdue invoices to the owner. Never contact the buyer about late payment without approval.
5. **Fulfillment.** Follow the normal order workflow. Partial shipments need the buyer's agreement where the client's terms require it.

## Stop gates

- Stop if the store's plan or app doesn't support the feature the request assumes.
- Prices, credit terms, tax exemptions and order edits after payment need owner approval.

## QA and handoff

Record the buyer, location, catalog or price list, terms, order or draft number, approval, and what was sent. Note which steps happened in Shopify and which are pending.
