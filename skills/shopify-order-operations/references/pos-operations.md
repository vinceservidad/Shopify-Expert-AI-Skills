# Shopify POS Operations

Use this when a store also sells in person with Shopify POS: in-store orders, returns and exchanges, stock transfers between store and warehouse, and end-of-day checks. POS features differ by plan (POS Lite or POS Pro) and by app version. Confirm the current labels on the merchant's device or admin before writing steps for staff.

## Prerequisites

- Locations that sell in person, and which location fulfills online orders.
- The merchant's policies for in-store returns, exchanges and refunds to the original payment method.
- Who on staff can do refunds, discounts and cash drawer actions.

## Ordered steps

1. **Stock by location.** POS sells from its own location's stock. Before an event or busy day, compare that location's stock with expected sales. Draft a transfer from the warehouse if needed, then confirm it arrived.
2. **Returns and exchanges.** Find the original order, online or in-store. Check that the return fits the policy, and restock only if the item can be resold. Refund to the original payment method unless policy says otherwise.
3. **End of day.** Compare the day's POS orders and payment totals with the cash drawer count and the card payout report. Record any difference with the staff member and time. Don't adjust totals to make them match.
4. **Online orders picked up in store.** Check pickup orders are marked ready only when packed, and marked picked up only when collected.

## Stop gates

- A cash or payment difference goes to the owner the same day.
- Stop if a refund exceeds the staff member's permission or the policy.

## Handoff

Location, date, orders and refunds reviewed, differences found, transfers drafted or received, and items waiting for the owner.
