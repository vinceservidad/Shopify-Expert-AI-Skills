# Google Ads Audit Checklist

## Business and economics

- primary business outcome, customer type, geography, seasonality, inventory, and capacity
- revenue, gross profit, or contribution-profit definition and included costs
- target or guardrail source and decision owner

## Measurement

- conversion actions, categories, sources, status, Primary versus Secondary setting, and account-default goals
- value, currency, counting, attribution model, windows, enhanced conversions, consent, and deduplication
- Shopify, GA4, Google Ads, payment, refund, and order-status reconciliation
- diagnostics, lag, modeled data, offline imports, and recent changes

## Account and campaign settings

- account timezone, currency, auto-apply settings, access, linked accounts, and change history
- campaign type, status, objective, networks, locations and location options, languages, schedule, devices, dates, and URLs
- budget, bid strategy, targets, portfolio strategy, experiments, and shared resources
- brand settings, new-customer settings, URL expansion, exclusions, and automation controls currently visible

## Search

- search terms, query intent, brand status, match type, keyword status, negatives, and conflicts
- ads, assets, policy, landing relevance, final URLs, tracking, and mobile experience
- impression share, rank and budget constraints, auction insights, and marginal query performance

## Shopping and Performance Max

- Merchant Center account, domain, data sources, countries, languages, destinations, shipping, tax, returns, and diagnostics
- item ID, title, description, product type, Google category, brand, GTIN or MPN, price, sale price, availability, images, variants, and custom labels
- disapprovals, warnings, price or availability mismatch, crawl issues, and policy state
- product, listing-group, asset-group, search-category, audience-signal, brand, placement, and landing evidence available in the account
- product-level spend, attributed value, realized outcomes, margin, stock, and returns

## Audiences and data

- observation versus targeting, lists, consent, membership duration, exclusions, customer match, and data freshness
- demographic and geographic performance with enough evidence for the decision

## Completeness statement

End with what was checked, what was unavailable, and which unavailable evidence could reverse the recommendation.
