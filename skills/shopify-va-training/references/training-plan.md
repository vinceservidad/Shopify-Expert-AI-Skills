## Progression

1. **Context:** store, customer, product, policies, commercial definitions, privacy, and authorization.
2. **Observation:** trainer demonstrates the current procedure and exception handling.
3. **Guided practice:** learner performs a sanitized or test task with prompts.
4. **Independent simulation:** learner completes realistic normal and exception cases without production effects.
5. **Supervised production:** learner performs an authorized low-risk task while an accountable reviewer checks it.
6. **Authorized independence:** owner grants the minimum role and tasks supported by evidence.
7. **Ongoing QA:** risk-based review, coaching, SOP updates, and access review.

## Assessment

Measure separately:

- source and policy knowledge
- correct task routing
- permission and authorization judgment
- field and process accuracy
- exception detection and escalation
- privacy and security
- QA and verification
- communication and handoff
- speed only after quality is stable

## Scenario design

Include missing data, conflicting sources, wrong store, duplicate product, unsupported claim, price mismatch, inventory ambiguity, already-refunded order, app dependency, permission failure, processing state, and failed publication. A learner who succeeds only on the happy path is not ready for unsupervised production work.
